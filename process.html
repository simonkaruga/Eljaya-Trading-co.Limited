At Eljaya Trading Co. in Nairobi, every branded merchandise order follows the same four-step process regardless of size. Whether you're ordering 20 polo shirts for a startup or 500 branded bottles for a corporate rollout, the process is the same because consistency is how we guarantee quality every time.

The discovery phase typically takes 2 to 3 days and involves a detailed brief covering your brand colours, logo files, target audience, and intended use. We ask the right questions upfront so there are no surprises later. From there, our design team produces digital mock-ups within 5 to 7 days, giving you a clear visual of exactly what your finished product will look like before a single item is produced.

Manufacturing takes 7 to 10 days depending on the product and quantity. We use premium materials sourced locally and internationally, with rigorous quality checks at each stage. Every order is inspected before dispatch. Delivery within Nairobi is included for most orders; upcountry delivery across Kenya is available on request.

The full process from brief to delivery typically runs 15 to 22 business days for standard orders. Rush turnarounds of 5 to 7 days are available for select products. Contact us to discuss your timeline and we will confirm what is achievable for your specific order.
