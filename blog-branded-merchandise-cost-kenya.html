How Much Does Branded Merchandise Cost in Kenya? 2026 Price Guide

One of the first questions we get from new clients is: "How much is this going to cost me?" It's a fair question, and honestly, the answer varies more than most people expect. The price of branded merchandise in Kenya depends on the product, the quantity you order, the printing method, and the quality of materials.

This guide breaks down real 2026 prices for the most popular branded merchandise categories we handle at Eljaya Trading Co. in Nairobi. No vague ranges — actual KSh figures you can use to plan your budget.

Corporate apparel is still the most requested item we produce. T-shirts and polo shirts get your brand seen everywhere — at events, in the office, on the street. The price difference between a basic tee and a quality polo is significant, so it's worth knowing what you're getting.

What Affects the Final Price?

The prices above are starting points. Here's what moves the needle up or down:

  • Quantity — the single biggest factor. Ordering 200 T-shirts costs significantly less per unit than ordering 30.
  • Number of print colours — screen printing charges per colour. A 3-colour logo costs more than a single-colour one.
  • Print method — screen printing is most economical for large runs; embroidery adds a premium feel but costs more; DTF (direct-to-film) is best for full-colour photographic designs.
  • Material quality — a 180gsm T-shirt and a 240gsm polo are very different products. The weight and fabric blend affect both feel and durability.
  • Turnaround time standard lead times are 7 to 14 days. Rush orders (3–5 days) carry a premium.
  • Delivery — we deliver within Nairobi; upcountry delivery is available at additional cost.
