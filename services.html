FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What branding services does Eljaya offer in Nairobi?

We offer corporate apparel, branded bottles, custom banners, safety reflectors, branded caps, eco-friendly jute bags, laundry bag manufacturing, custom packaging, brand collateral and corporate stationery.

How much does branded merchandise cost in Nairobi?

Prices vary by product and quantity. As a guide: branded t-shirts start from KSh 650 per piece (100+ pieces), branded water bottles from KSh 450, jute bags from KSh 280, and custom banners from KSh 1,800. Larger orders attract bulk discounts. Contact us for a free itemised quote.

How long does branded merchandise production take?

Standard production takes 7–14 days. Our process: Discovery (2–3 days), Design (5–7 days), Production (7–10 days), Delivery (1–2 days). Rush orders are available on request.

Can I order a small batch under 20 pieces?

Yes. Minimum orders start from 1 piece for banners, 20 pieces for apparel, 24 pieces for caps and 25 pieces for bottles. Small batch orders are welcome contact us and we will advise on the best options for your budget.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes. We deliver branded merchandise across Kenya including Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret and other towns via courier. Delivery timelines and costs depend on location and order size.

What file format do you need for my logo or artwork?

We prefer vector files: AI, EPS or PDF with fonts outlined. High-resolution PNG or JPEG (minimum 300 DPI) also works for most print jobs. If you only have a low-resolution logo, our design team can redraw it in vector format for a small fee.

Do you provide samples before bulk production?

Yes. For most products we provide a physical sample or digital proof before proceeding to bulk production. Sample lead times are typically 2–3 business days, ensuring you are fully satisfied with colour, print quality and sizing before we produce the full order.

Do you offer eco-friendly branding solutions?

Yes. We specialise in eco-friendly packaging and sustainable merchandise including jute bags, biodegradable packaging and environmentally responsible branded products across Kenya.
